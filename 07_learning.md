# AI and Human Learning

At the end of this section you will have:
- a framework to think about critical thinking and struggle
- strategies to improve your software for research skills
- given feedback about the afternoon

As a senior: AI amplifies strengths, be the architect

---

As a medior: concentrate on code review and QA (quality assurance), boundaries, use it as a learning opportunity for more advanced topics such as performance optimisation, architecture, etc.

---

As a junior: use AI to learn, ask the AI ‘why’, only use AI generated code if you can explain it to someone else, practice without AI sometimes, focus on testing, verification and maintainability

All: practice prompting

# Next steps

Experiment, share your findings, contribute to tools, advocate for positive change, keep a lifelong student mentality, balance enthusiasm with prudence, mentor the next generation
